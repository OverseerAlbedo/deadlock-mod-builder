# Code of conduct

This project is a small modding tool with a small community. Everyone who takes part in it, in issues, pull requests, discussions or the support channels, agrees to the following.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient. Many people here are new to modding, Blender or Rust; a question that looks obvious to you is not obvious to them.
- Give precise, reproducible reports and reviews: versions, steps, files, what you expected and what happened.
- Credit the authors of models, textures and tools you use, and respect their licences. Do not post or request game dumps or third-party character assets.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Keep private conversations private.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or sexualised language or imagery, in any channel connected to the project.
- Publishing someone's private information, including Steam accounts, e-mail addresses or files from their computer, without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or pressuring maintainers for attention or releases.

## Enforcement

The maintainer (@OverseerAlbedo) may edit, hide or remove contributions, lock threads and block accounts that break these rules, and will explain the decision when asked. Report a problem privately to the maintainer; reports are handled confidentially.

## Scope

This applies to every space the project runs (the GitHub repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions) and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.
